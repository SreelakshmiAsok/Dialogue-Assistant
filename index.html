📋 How to Facilitate

1

Choose a scenario from "Start Session" that fits today's learning goal.

2

Sit beside your child. Let them watch the character ask the question out loud.

3

Tap the microphone or AAC picture cards to help your child respond.

4

Review Buddy's feedback card together and celebrate correct responses!

🔬 Dataset & Reasoning Engine

Our system integrates an OWL Ontology Reasoning Engine (`social_communication.owl`) with FastAPI backend (`http://localhost:8000/reason`), combined with linguistic datasets:
• TamilMixSentiment – code-mixed Tamil-English corpus
• DravidianLangTech 2022 – slang & sentiment tagging
• AI4Bharat IndicCorp V2 – Tamil pragmatics baseline

👁️ Attention Detection

During gameplay, the app uses the device webcam to detect if the child looks away for more than 5 seconds. Buddy the Mascot will gently call them back!

Requires camera permission — no video is stored or transmitted.
